Read progress at the scale of the experiment

Whole-brain structure, local circuit function, and embodied behaviour are different achievements. Compare their scope and tests rather than assigning a single completion percentage.

How far do the wiring maps extend?

StudyScope and resultWhat it does not establish
Cook et al., 2019Whole-animal connectomes of both C. elegans sexes.[1]Reproduction of all behaviours and states.
Winding et al., 2023Larval fly brain, 3,016 neurons.[2]A direct scaling rule to adults or humans.
FlyWire, 2024Adult female fly brain, 139,255 neurons.[3]Readout of the animal's learning history.
MICrONS, 2025Paired structure and activity in a mouse visual-cortex volume.[4]Complete functional emulation of a mouse brain.
BANC / MaleCNS, 2026Separate datasets integrating adult brain and ventral nerve cord.[5][6]Reproduction of all individual-specific neural states and memories.

Cell counts, neuron counts, synapse counts, and imaging volumes are different quantities. A whole brain, a whole central nervous system, and a local volume have different boundaries. Reconstruction errors also remain possible; record the dataset version and proofreading scope.

Predicting function from connectivity

Shiu et al. (2024) used a connectome-based fly brain model to investigate sensorimotor circuits and reported experimentally checked predictions. Lappalainen et al. (2024) optimized parameters under visual-connectivity and motion-detection task constraints, comparing predictions with neural measurements across 26 studies. The latter uses a task objective as well as connectivity.[7][8]

A mouse visual-cortex foundation model tested generalization to new animals and stimulus domains. This advances response prediction without establishing recovery of whole-brain mechanisms or individual memories.[9]

Interpreting embodied models

BAAIWorm closes the loop between a C. elegans nervous-system model, body, and environment to reproduce locomotion. This tests the sensory–motor–sensory cycle, an important direction for emulation research.[11]

Eon Systems' 2026 fly demonstration integrates existing brain, vision, and body models. Its technical description identifies manually selected descending-neuron mappings, imitation-learned controllers, and missing validation of internal dynamics. Carboncopies also questions whether the evidence warrants the term “upload.” This guide treats the result as embodied model integration, distinct from recovery of an individual's memories and complete internal state.[12][13]

What neural interfaces demonstrate

Speech and motor neuroprostheses show how useful function can be decoded from limited neural signals. Speech decoding, continuous voice generation, and brain–spine interfaces were evaluated in different tasks and participants. Their relevance includes closed-loop testing, long-term stability, and individual adaptation rather than whole-brain readout.[14][15][16]

Hippocampal prostheses also investigate modelling and intervention for bounded memory tasks. These results do not demonstrate transfer of arbitrary memories to another person or machine.[17]

Reading the 2026 record

The peer-reviewed BANC paper, the MaleCNS paper and public data, and the autumn 2026 larval CNS “first draft” have different publication statuses. The last item is a preprint. Track preprint release, journal publication, and dataset updates separately.[5][6][18][19]

This account reflects sources checked on 4 November 2026. Larger datasets matter, but they do not remove the need for functional validation.

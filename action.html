Research you can start now

Use public data and reproducible tests to determine which information is necessary and what remains missing.

Four concrete projects

Identification

Which observations are necessary?

Vary observation count and noise in synthetic circuits; compare responses to held-out interventions. Report the relationship between measurement budget and error.[1]

Reconstruction

What do wiring errors break?

Add controlled missing and false connections to a public connectome and measure predictive changes. Distinguish injected errors from known dataset errors.[2]

Prediction

Does it generalize?

Reproduce models and simple baselines on resources such as ZAPBench. Test robustness to time, stimulus, and session splits.[3]

Mechanism

How much does connectivity help?

Compare activity-only, true-connectivity, and shuffled-connectivity models with matched tasks and computational budgets.[4]

These are proposed projects, not results already obtained by this site.

Specify the deliverable first

  1. Select a paper and freeze the data version, usage conditions, and target figure or table.
  2. Run a minimal baseline and check the data split and metric.
  3. Change one hypothesis and compare on independent evaluation conditions.
  4. Save configuration, environment, runtime, seeds, successes, and failures.
  5. Publish explainable differences separately from unresolved discrepancies.

Platforms such as NEURON and the Brain Modeling ToolKit help implement models. Using a simulator does not itself establish biological validity.[5][6]

Where different skills contribute

Experimental neuroscience: structure–activity registration, interventions, longitudinal recordings. Microscopy and image analysis: throughput, deformation correction, segmentation, proofreading. Mathematics and machine learning: inverse problems, identifiability, uncertainty, generalization. Computing: memory, distributed execution, reproducibility. Literature curation: maintain links among DOI, version, corrections, data, and code.

Turn the ambition of running a brain into small, falsifiable deliverables that resolve a necessary part of the problem.

Choose one experiment from your strengths

The three routes can advance in parallel. These are this site’s research proposals. Make the first deliverable a reproduced result, one changed condition and a comparison with the original.

Wiring and data analysis

Run a public fly model and measure how added wiring errors alter predictions. Deliver a comparison and reproducible code that inform acquisition accuracy.[1][2]

Inference and model comparison

Vary measurements and noise in synthetic circuits with known truth. Test which additional measurements distinguish candidate models, then report acquisition priorities.[3][4]

Computation and body models

Match body-side conditions, then vary the circuit or feedback delay and compare behaviour. Establish operating conditions for stable, long-running comparisons.[5][6][7]

Make a small result useful to the next step: record data and model versions, changed conditions, separation of training and evaluation data, results and failure conditions. A result that others can reproduce and use as the basis for another experiment extends the research path.

Reproduce one prediction, then test required wiring accuracy

Priority project: use a public fly connectome model to test how wiring errors change predictions that were experimentally validated. Begin with one sensorimotor task and a small circuit, reproducing the original study first. Shiu and colleagues' model is a candidate; Lappalainen and colleagues' visual model offers a possible independent modelling framework.[1][2]

This is a proposed experiment, not a report of comparisons performed here.

Without paired functional data, individual specificity remains untested.

Left–right differences are within-individual controls, not substitutes for individual variation. Without paired structure and activity from the same individual, report connectome-model robustness rather than individual emulation.

Correspondence and confounds in C4: Schlegel and colleagues provide evidence on cell-type matching and structural variation between FlyWire and hemibrain. Comparisons including BANC and MaleCNS must document treatment of inputs outside shared regions, sex, developmental stage, cell counts, coverage, data versions, reconstruction completeness, transmitter predictions and matching uncertainty. Exclude unmatched types by prespecified rules and vary these factors in sensitivity analyses. Do not treat the four connectomes as homogeneous biological replicates.[8][9][10][11]

Relationship to prior work: building on comparisons of cell types and structure, we propose measuring prediction changes under common model rules, inputs and metrics. Structural or simulated variability can inform margin selection but does not determine δ or ε: use functional repeatability and task-relevant differences to define those separately. Follow the statistical procedure and establish the contribution beyond existing sensitivity analyses before running the experiment.

Separate preparation, completion and success

  1. Prepare: record data/code versions, reuse terms, circuit scope, the target figure or table and inputs. Narrow the experiment if required data are unavailable.
  2. Pilot: measure memory, runtime and storage; budget all conditions and multiple seeds. Do not infer runtime from paper scale alone.
  3. Prespecify decisions: choose the metric, tolerance, minimum effect, error range, seeds and a date for reviewing the approach.
  4. Completion: publish C0 reproduction and feasible C1–C4 comparisons, including negative results. Identify comparisons that could not be performed.
  5. Success: C0 exceeds suitable controls by a prespecified meaningful difference, and a prediction-preserving error range is confirmed in another model. Without a biological tolerance, label the result a provisional sensitivity analysis.

If C0 cannot be reproduced, investigate versions and preprocessing before reporting a reproducibility problem. If scores never change, examine metric sensitivity; if tiny errors collapse performance, investigate model instability first.

Follow-up projects and supporting tools

After bounding required accuracy, follow-up projects can examine measurement choice in known artificial circuits, held-out activity prediction in ZAPBench, and paired structure–function measurements. Choose a platform for the question.[4][12][13]

NEURON supports cellular and circuit simulation; the Brain Modeling ToolKit supports multiple modelling scales; SpiNNaker provides a reference architecture for communicating large numbers of spikes. Distinguish what the papers evaluated from the capabilities of the software version used in a new experiment.[5][6][14]

Where different skills contribute

Experimental neuroscience: structure–activity registration, interventions, longitudinal recordings. Microscopy and image analysis: throughput, deformation correction, segmentation, proofreading. Mathematics and machine learning: inverse problems, identifiability, uncertainty, generalization. Computing: memory, distributed execution, reproducibility. Literature curation: maintain links among DOI, version, corrections, data, and code.

Turn the ambition of running a brain into small, falsifiable deliverables that resolve a necessary part of the problem.

Evidence needed to advance

StageRequired outputExample advancement criterion
MeasurementRegistered structure, activity, and molecular dataQuantify missingness and distortion.
ReconstructionCells and connections with proofreading provenanceMeasure how structural errors affect downstream results.

ValidationIndependent tests in the same individualEvaluate retained functions against prespecified criteria.

This staged plan is an editorial research proposal informed by the 2008 roadmap, the 2025 review, and current Carboncopies updates.[1][2][3]

Explore imaging and computation scale

What faster computers do not solve

Compute is one requirement. Faster hardware cannot recover unmeasured information simply by running longer. Segmentation, registration, parameter identifiability, learning rules, and independent evaluation data remain linked challenges.[4][5][6]

Tracking demonstrated capabilities is more useful for research planning than assigning one arrival date. Extrapolation to humans must state differences in species, region, learning history, and observable timescales.

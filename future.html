The history page brings together six domains and 12 open tasks and five stages targeting 2050. This page explains the M1–M4 tests underlying the early stages and how to estimate computational scale.

Four milestones and decision rules

Break the long-term goal into testable milestones. The following is this site's proposed strategy, informed by the 2008 roadmap, a recent technical review and Carboncopies' update programme; decisions belong to individual experiments.[1][2][3]

MilestoneRequired comparison and outputDecision to advancePresent evidence
M1: audit reconstruction methodsKnown artificial circuits; publish observation, noise and reconstruction-error curves.Meet prespecified criteria on unseen circuits and interventions, reproduced by another implementation.Identification studies and the Challenge framework provide starting points.[4][5]
M2: bound required wiring accuracyPublic models, such as fly models; vary missing/false connections and measure prediction changes.Reproduce the original result and confirm an error range that preserves predictions within a prespecified tolerance in a second model.Connectome-based predictions exist; a shared error tolerance is not established.[6][7]
M3: establish individual correspondencePaired structure, activity and interventions; other-individual and average-model controls.A prespecified advantage δ on unseen interventions that cannot be explained by measurement error.MICrONS provides paired structure and activity, not completion of this entire criterion.[8]
M4: test previously learned informationDifferently trained and control individuals; independent recall after reconstruction.Prespecified correspondence in retention, forgetting and relearning without training the model on the test answers.Unachieved in this review's scope. Engram research helps identify candidate measurements.[9]

See the validation protocol for setting δ and tolerances. M4 alone would not certify completed WBE; it addresses claims about an individual's memory.

Change direction when evidence changes

Working hypothesis: test whether structure and molecular information surviving fixation retain enough information about the long-term memories and individual differences of interest. Preserved anatomy and expert opinion do not establish sufficiency.[10][11]

  • If structure alone fails on intervention predictions, add functional or molecular observations and measure the benefit.
  • If individual wiring does not outperform an average model, inspect task sensitivity and model form. Report the possibility that average information is sufficient for the tested generic function.
  • If a method succeeds on artificial circuits but fails on biology, revise the synthetic-data assumptions.

Iterate between artificial circuits, small animals and local circuits, then test whether correspondence survives greater anatomical coverage. A sequence of species is not a guarantee or a timetable for human WBE.

Explore imaging and computation scale

Separate imaging volume from simulation cost. The calculator accepts anisotropic voxels. For example, 1 mm³ at 10×10×10 nm and one byte per voxel gives 1 PB of raw images. This is a geometric example, not an estimate of required resolution or actual stored data.

Compression, indexing, communication, redundancy, preprocessing, proofreading, training and intracellular compartment calculations are excluded. Cost estimates require method- and sample-specific measurements.[12]

What faster computers do not solve

Compute is one requirement. Faster hardware cannot recover unmeasured information simply by running longer. Segmentation, registration, parameter identifiability, learning rules, and independent evaluation data remain linked challenges.[12][13][4]

Tracking demonstrated capabilities is more useful for research planning than assigning one arrival date. Extrapolation to humans must state differences in species, region, learning history, and observable timescales.

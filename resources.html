Site source and revision history

This is a personal research guide written and edited by yasufumi-nakata. Public Carboncopies Foundation material is used as cited source material. The site is not issued as an official Foundation website, statement, supervised publication or endorsed document.

Send questions or correction requests through GitHub Issues, identifying the page, section, statement and supporting evidence. Verified corrections are reflected in the text and revision record; unresolved evidence is marked as uncertain. The complete change history provides differences from earlier versions.

Search scope and citation method

The collection began with Carboncopies' public library, FAQ and research pages, Strout's historical resource and the previous site's references, extending into measurement, system identification, functional prediction and neuroprosthetics. For the 6 November 2026 update, 20 DOIs raised by Issues #282–292 were queried in Scopus and bibliographic records for 19 results were saved. Full texts were then acquired from publishers, PMC and university repositories, with relevant sources reviewed before adoption. A Scopus match or download notification alone was not treated as full-text review. This was neither adoption of every candidate nor an exhaustive database search. Philosophy and ethics are outside this guide's scope.[10][11][12]

Citations are numbered within each page. Records include authors, title, year, venue, volume/issue/pages or article number, and DOI or source URL. Long author lists display six names followed by “et al.” Web resources have access dates; peer-reviewed papers, preprints, technical reports and organisational statements are distinguished.

Full-text review covers the saved PDF or the cited webpage, not separate supplementary files that were not acquired. For a book chapter, the chapter scope is stated. Proposed experiments are explicitly identified as proposals, separate from original findings.

This is a scoped literature guide, not an exhaustive systematic review. The public site links to legitimate distribution sources rather than redistributing full texts.

Changes in this edition

2026-11-06: In response to Issues #282–292, this update adds throughput conditions, glial and extrasynaptic mechanisms, individual comparisons, approximate posteriors, statistical decision procedures and human reference-data requirements. It also revises historical milestones, synapse-versus-edge counts, author records and BibTeX, and specifies the personal site’s editorial, citation and reuse policies.

The 1986 starting point is attributed through the reviewed Cook et al. paper, not presented as a new full-text review of the White et al. original. The Markram et al. (2015) candidate remains outside the reviewed catalogue and direct timeline citations because its full-text review was not completed in this update.[13]

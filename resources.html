Search scope and citation method

The search began with Carboncopies' public Zotero collection, its FAQ, research and roadmap pages, Strout's historical resource, and the previous site's references. Bibliographic records and access were checked against publishers, author versions, Crossref, and Europe PMC. Additions emphasize 2024–2026 connectomes, structure–function studies, microscopy, activity prediction, and neural interfaces. Works primarily about philosophy, ethics, fiction, or weakly related topics were excluded.[10][11][12]

The guide uses numbered citations. Records include authors, title, year, venue, available volume/issue/pages or article number, and DOI or source URL. Long author lists display the first six followed by “et al.”; JSON and BibTeX retain the retrieved author lists. Web resources include an access date, and preprints are distinguished from peer-reviewed articles.

This is not an exhaustive systematic review. Scope, selection, and retrieval gaps are recorded for future updates. The public site links to legitimate distribution sources rather than redistributing third-party full texts.

Terms that should remain distinct

Connectome
A representation of neural connectivity within a specified boundary. Check resolution and the coverage of chemical and electrical synapses.
Simulation
Computing the behaviour of an assumed model.
Emulation
Reproduction aimed at functional correspondence to a target system, requiring an explicit definition and tests of correspondence.
System identification
Estimating model structure or parameters from observed inputs, outputs, and related measurements.
Identifiability
The ability to distinguish candidate models using the available observations.
Engram
A concept for studying physical changes and cell ensembles associated with memory retention and recall; not a complete codebook for arbitrary experiences.
Digital twin
A broad term that needs a specified individual, measurement scope, update rule, and validation task.

Search scope and citation method

The collection began with Carboncopies' public library, FAQ and research pages, Strout's historical resource and the previous site's references, extending into measurement, system identification, functional prediction and neuroprosthetics. This revision matched supplied full texts to the inventory and excluded remaining unavailable items. Philosophy and ethics are outside this guide's scope.[10][11][12]

Citations are numbered within each page. Records include authors, title, year, venue, volume/issue/pages or article number, and DOI or source URL. Long author lists display six names followed by “et al.” Web resources have access dates; peer-reviewed papers, preprints, technical reports and organisational statements are distinguished.

Full-text review covers the saved PDF or the cited webpage, not separate supplementary files that were not acquired. For a book chapter, the chapter scope is stated. Proposed experiments are explicitly identified as proposals, separate from original findings.

This is a scoped literature guide, not an exhaustive systematic review. The public site links to legitimate distribution sources rather than redistributing full texts.

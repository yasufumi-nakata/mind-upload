Current research pillars

Carboncopies Foundation supports research toward substrate-independent minds, with WBE as a technical pathway. Its research page emphasizes converting measurements into executable models and validating the results. This page is an independent account of public information, not an official Foundation page.[1][2]

Brain Emulation Challenge

Generate virtual measurements from known circuits and compare reconstruction methods.

BrainGenix

A development platform for models, execution, and experiments; its existence does not establish a finished upload system.

WBE Roadmap

An ongoing update of technical requirements and research needs, building on the 2008 roadmap.

What the Brain Emulation Challenge tests

We cannot know every true parameter of a biological brain. A synthetic circuit allows ground truth to be retained while participants receive only observations. The Challenge reconstructs models from virtual structural and activity measurements and compares structure, dynamics, and behavioural or input–output performance.[3]

  1. Fix the ground-truth circuit and task, reserving evaluation data.
  2. Vary resolution, noise, missingness, and measurement cost.
  3. Compare reconstruction methods under matched information budgets.
  4. Test assumptions that worked in synthetic circuits against biological data.

These steps are this guide's experimental design proposal informed by the official concept. Success on synthetic ground truth does not guarantee that every necessary biological state variable was represented.

Roadmap publication status

On 4 November 2026, the official roadmap listed published work on fidelity and microscopy, with inverse problems and proof-of-concept systems listed as upcoming topics. It is an ongoing research programme.[4]

Use the official research resources

Start with BrainGenix, the Challenge overview, and the roadmap. Check the currently released experiments and software requirements; capabilities and execution environments can change.[7][3]

The public Zotero library links direct WBE work with system identification, neural prosthetics, memory, and measurement research. It was one starting point for this guide's literature search. Inclusion in the library does not itself validate a paper's conclusions or the feasibility of uploading.[8]

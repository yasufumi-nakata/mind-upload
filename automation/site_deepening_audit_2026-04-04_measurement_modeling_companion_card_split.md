# Summary

The selected weakness was a **category error at the measurement-model boundary**.

[`wiki/measurement-and-modeling-terms.md`](/Users/yasufumi/Documents/GitHub/mind-upload/wiki/measurement-and-modeling-terms.md) already taught the basic flow:

- observation
- organization
- estimation
- verification

It also already separated:

- inverse-problem gates
- DCM axes
- identifiability-driven experiment design

But one scientifically important ambiguity still remained:

- a reader could still slide from `same-session multimodal`
- or `same-subject proxy-rich`
- or `same-brain sequential`

to the vague thought that **measurement itself had simply become stronger**.

That is no longer a safe simplification under the current primary literature.

# Why This Needed Correction

## 1. A single route card no longer covers the failure mode

The page already told readers to ask for a `route card`.

That was directionally right, but now too weak.

Why:

- a route card explains one measurement or estimation route
- but several current overreads happen **between routes or stages**

Those are not single-route failures.

They are relation failures:

- fusion failure
- human-proxy composition failure
- state-continuity failure

Therefore the page needed to separate:

- `route card`
- `companion card`

instead of leaving both inside one generic verification bucket.

## 2. Same-session multimodal is not one solved measurement object

- [Kothe et al. (2025)](https://doi.org/10.1162/IMAG.a.136) show that synchronized multimodal recording infrastructure does not itself establish device-side delay truth.
- [Vafaii et al. (2024)](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-023-44363-z) show that simultaneous modalities reveal both common and divergent organization.
- [Chen et al. (2025)](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-025-64414-x) show tightly coupled global progression together with distinct multimodal network patterns.
- [Bolt et al. (2025)](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41593-025-01945-y) show substantial autonomic coupling of a major global fMRI mode.
- [Epp et al. (2025)](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41593-025-02132-9) show that BOLD changes can oppose oxygen-metabolism changes.

Critique:

- `shared clock` is not `shared temporal object`
- `shared factor` is not `target biological variable`
- `same session` is not `solved quantity bridge`

Therefore the page needed to say explicitly that this is a `Fusion Card` problem, not just a stronger route card.

## 3. Several living-human proxy rows are not one same-subject state sample

- [Li et al. (2025)](https://doi.org/10.1093/pnasnexus/pgaf072) constrain a dynamic deuterium kinetic route.
- [Bøgh et al. (2024)](https://doi.org/10.1186/s41747-024-00426-4) show route-local repeatability at a specific DMI operating point.
- [Morgan et al. (2024)](https://doi.org/10.1002/nbm.5256) show materially different BBB water-exchange estimates across ASL route families.
- [Amiri et al. (2023)](https://doi.org/10.1093/brain/awac335) show how direct multimodal comparison can shrink to a restricted complete-feature subset.
- [Manasova et al. (2026)](https://doi.org/10.1093/brain/awaf412) show improved multimodal performance together with higher inter-modality disagreement in hard groups.

Critique:

- `real human data` is not yet `one reusable row`
- `same subject` is not yet `one state axis`
- `multiple rows` are not yet `one valid bundle`

The page therefore needed to name that this is a `Human Proxy Composition Card` problem rather than generic route-card disclosure.

## 4. Same-brain and same-subject bridges do not automatically preserve the carried object

- [Bosch et al. (2022)](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-022-30199-6) show correlative in vivo-to-structure bridges with explicit local witness objects.
- [MICrONS Consortium et al. (2025)](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-025-08790-w) show powerful same-brain structure-function linkage while still not producing one global same-state object.
- [Gallego et al. (2020)](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41593-019-0555-4) show stable latent dynamics under consistent behavior.
- [Van De Ville et al. (2021)](https://doi.org/10.1126/sciadv.abj0751) show temporality of fingerprint features rather than one timeless state witness.
- [Karpowicz et al. (2025)](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-025-59652-y), [Wilson et al. (2025)](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41551-025-01536-z), and [Wairagkar et al. (2025)](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-025-09127-3) show why stable use can depend on alignment, recalibration, or bounded fixed-horizon support.

Critique:

- `same brain` is not yet `same state`
- `stable score` is not yet `same carried object`
- `usable over time` is not yet `continuity without rescue`

The page therefore needed to say explicitly that this is a `State-Continuity Bridge Card` problem.

# Root Critique

The scientific criticism is precise:

1. The page already separated measurement, estimation, and verification.
2. But it still under-separated **single-route audits** from **cross-route / cross-stage audits**.
3. Current primary literature does not support reading multimodal fusion, human-proxy composition, and sequential same-brain bridging as one generic increase in evidence strength.
4. Therefore the page had to distinguish `route card` from `companion card`.

The corrected rule is now:

`single-route ceiling -> route card`

`relation among routes or stages -> companion card`

with the companion-card family split made explicit as:

- `Fusion Card`
- `Human Proxy Composition Card`
- `State-Continuity Bridge Card`

# Summary

The selected weakness was a **learning-portal lag at the route-family layer for living-human evidence**.

The core public pages and technical wiki pages had already become much stricter about current human observability:

- route family first
- proxy class second
- operational maturity third
- calibrator role fourth
- bundle composition and bridge validity after that

However, [`wiki/index.md`](/Users/yasufumi/Documents/GitHub/mind-upload/wiki/index.md) still summarized the entrance rule mainly as a `three-axis + bridge/composition` reading. That was no longer strong enough. For newer readers, the page still left too much room to start from an overbroad category such as `human proxy evidence` and only later discover that the compared rows did not even constrain the same inferential object.

# Why This Needed Correction

## 1. Human spectroscopy is already split before any shared three-axis reading is allowed

- [Lucchetti et al. (2025)](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-025-66124-w) define a `five-metabolite parcel-similarity graph` from whole-brain `1H-MRSI`.
- [Li et al. (2025)](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40109558/) define `dynamic deuterium kinetic-rate maps` under a blood-input kinetic model.
- [Bøgh et al. (2024)](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38472611/) show route-local `3 T repeatability` under a specific glucose protocol and time-point window.

Critique:

- `human metabolic imaging` is not one route family.
- `metabolic connectome`, `absolute deuterium metabolite burden`, and `kinetic-rate imaging` do not share one quantity type.
- Therefore the learning portal must teach readers to fix the route family before comparing maturity or calibrator role.

## 2. The blood-CSF-barrier lane is not one support-state row

- [Zhao et al. (2020)](https://doi.org/10.1186/s12987-020-00218-z) constrain `choroid-plexus perfusion`.
- [Petitclerc et al. (2021)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7612938/) constrain `blood-to-CSF water transport`.
- [Anderson et al. (2022)](https://doi.org/10.1080/21688370.2021.1963143) constrain `choroid-plexus water cycling`.
- [Petitclerc et al. (2026)](https://doi.org/10.1177/0271678X261429042) constrain `simultaneous BBB-versus-BCSFB exchange`.

Critique:

- Those papers do not share one crossed boundary, one transport object, or one model family.
- A beginner page that says only `human barrier route` teaches a scientifically weaker model than the rest of the site now permits.

## 3. Astrocyte-related and neuroimmune PET are already target-defined families, not one glial PET lane

- [Villemagne et al. (2022)](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35086898/) constrain a `first-in-human SMBT-1 MAO-B target-validation` route.
- [Matsuoka et al. (2026)](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40913132/) constrain a `simplified SL25.1188 quantification` route in AD.
- [Tyacke et al. (2018)](https://doi.org/10.2967/jnumed.118.208009) constrain a separate `I2BS` route family.
- [Biechele et al. (2023)](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-023-40937-z) show that `TSPO` should not be read in humans as a rodent-style activated-microglia marker.
- [Ogata et al. (2025)](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39746757/) constrain a first-in-human `CSF1R` route.
- [Yan et al. (2025)](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39848764/) constrain an `enzyme-defined COX-2` route.

Critique:

- `astrocyte PET` is not one quantity.
- `neuroimmune PET` is not one quantity either.
- `SMBT-1`, `SL25.1188`, `I2BS`, `TSPO`, `CSF1R`, and `COX-2` cannot be averaged into one generic `glial-support` or `immune-state` lane before target family is fixed.

# Root Critique

The scientific criticism is precise:

1. The learning portal still let the user start one step too late.
2. `proxy class / operational maturity / calibrator role` are meaningful only after the route family has already been typed.
3. Without that order, a beginner can still compare unlike objects as if they were one ladder:
   - `1H-MRSI similarity` versus `deuterium kinetic-rate imaging`
   - `choroid-plexus perfusion` versus `blood-to-CSF transport`
   - `SMBT-1` versus `SL25.1188` versus `I2BS`
   - `TSPO` versus `CSF1R` versus `COX-2`
4. That mistake matters because it weakens every later claim-ceiling judgment built on the entrance model.

Therefore the correct beginner rule is:

`route family first -> three-axis reading second -> bundle / bridge audit after that`

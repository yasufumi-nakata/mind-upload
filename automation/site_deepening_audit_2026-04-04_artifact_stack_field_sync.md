# Site Deepening Audit (2026-04-04, artifact-stack field-level sync)

## Target

- Main target:
  - `wiki/baselines-prereg-and-model-cards.md`
- Scope:
  - technical and natural-science only
- Explicitly excluded:
  - philosophy
  - law
  - institutional design

## Why this was the next page to deepen

The site had already done the hard conceptual work.

- `Verification` had become explicit about:
  - `Fusion Card`
  - `Human Proxy Composition Card`
  - `State-Continuity Bridge Card`
- The deeper wiki pages had also become stricter about:
  - effective-window mismatch and shared-vs-specific multimodal structure
  - human proxy role, maturity, calibrator role, and disagreement topology
  - carried object, tolerance rule, and rescue route in sequential bridges

But the learning guide at `wiki/baselines-prereg-and-model-cards.md` still described those cards too briefly.

That was now a scientific weakness, not just a documentation mismatch. If the learning guide says only:

- `Fusion Card`
- `Human Proxy Composition Card`
- `State-Continuity Bridge Card`

without naming the fields that now matter, it leaves an operational loophole. A reader can think the card is present even when the scientifically important burden is still under-specified.

## Main conclusion

The next weakness on the site was no longer card count. It was **card sufficiency**.

Three cards had become too abbreviated in the learning guide:

1. `Fusion Card`
2. `Human Proxy Composition Card`
3. `State-Continuity Bridge Card`

The current primary literature does not support short-label readings of those cards. Each now needs field-level disclosure to block a specific kind of scientific overreading.

## Evidence-based criticism

### 1. Fusion cannot be reduced to synchronization plus co-registration

The older guide still made `Fusion Card` sound close to:

- acquisition relation
- lag audit
- co-registration
- fusion model
- baseline

That is now too weak.

- [Kothe et al. (2025)](https://doi.org/10.1162/IMAG.a.136) show that synchronized multimodal recording does not by itself certify device-side delay truth or biological equivalence.
- [Vafaii et al. (2024)](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-023-44363-z) show that simultaneous modalities can reveal both common and divergent organization.
- [Chen et al. (2025)](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-025-64414-x) show coupled global progression together with distinct multimodal network patterns in simultaneous EEG-PET-MRI.
- [Bolt et al. (2025)](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41593-025-01945-y) show that a major global fMRI mode is substantially coupled to autonomic physiology.
- [Epp et al. (2025)](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41593-025-02132-9) show that significant BOLD changes can oppose oxygen-metabolism changes.
- [Rohaut et al. (2024)](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41591-024-03019-1) and [Manasova et al. (2026)](https://doi.org/10.1093/brain/awaf412) show that multimodal gains can coexist with uncertainty, disagreement, and hard-subgroup mismatch.

Criticism:

- `simultaneous` is not yet `one temporal object`
- `shared factor` is not yet `the target biological variable`
- `more modalities` is not yet `robust multimodal evidence`

Therefore the learning guide had to require:

- effective-window / temporal-kernel relation
- shared-vs-specific component disclosure
- quantity bridge / physiology grounding
- complete-case and missing-modality disclosure
- transfer / disagreement window

### 2. Human proxy composition cannot be reduced to proxy class plus model burden

The older guide still made `Human Proxy Composition Card` sound close to:

- proxy class
- direct observable
- same-subject relation
- model burden
- increment over strongest row

That is now too weak.

- [Li et al. (2025)](https://doi.org/10.1093/pnasnexus/pgaf072) show a kinetic deuterium route rather than a generic metabolic row.
- [Bøgh et al. (2024)](https://doi.org/10.1186/s41747-024-00426-4) show that deuterium repeatability is an operating-point property, not a route-free guarantee.
- [Morgan et al. (2024)](https://doi.org/10.1002/nbm.5256) show that BBB water-exchange estimates depend materially on ASL route choice.
- [Vafaii et al. (2024)](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-023-44363-z) and [Chen et al. (2025)](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-025-64414-x) show that cross-modal agreement can mix shared and divergent structure.
- [Manasova et al. (2026)](https://doi.org/10.1093/brain/awaf412) show higher inter-modality disagreement in clinically difficult groups.

Criticism:

- `real human data` is not yet `one common row`
- `same subject` is not yet `one state axis`
- `multiple rows` are not yet `one valid bundle`

Therefore the learning guide had to require:

- evidence role by row
- effective time window / state axis
- physiological / perturbation regime compatibility
- operational maturity
- calibrator role
- method-family non-equivalence
- agreement / disagreement topology and resolution policy

### 3. State continuity cannot be reduced to elapsed time plus validation rung

The older guide still made `State-Continuity Bridge Card` sound close to:

- acquisition order
- elapsed time
- regime continuity
- coordinate transfer
- validation rung

That is now too weak.

- [Bosch et al. (2022)](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-022-30199-6) and [MICrONS Consortium et al. (2025)](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-025-08790-w) show that strong same-brain pipelines still carry local witnesses rather than one global same-state object.
- [Gallego et al. (2020)](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41593-019-0555-4) and [Van De Ville et al. (2021)](https://doi.org/10.1126/sciadv.abj0751) show that what can remain stable depends on the object carried across time.
- [Karpowicz et al. (2025)](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-025-59652-y), [Wilson et al. (2025)](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41551-025-01536-z), and [Wairagkar et al. (2025)](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-025-09127-3) show why performance stability can depend on alignment, recalibration, or limited-horizon support rather than raw continuity.

Criticism:

- `same-subject` is not yet `same-state`
- `stable score` is not yet `same object persisted`
- `bridge passed` is not yet `failure criteria were explicit`

Therefore the learning guide had to require:

- carried object / bridge witness
- tolerance / failure rule
- rescue route versus raw continuity

- `Fusion Card present` = enough, even if temporal mismatch or disagreement topology is missing
- `human proxy bundle present` = enough, even if row role or method non-equivalence is missing
- `same-subject bridge present` = enough, even if carried object and failure rule are absent

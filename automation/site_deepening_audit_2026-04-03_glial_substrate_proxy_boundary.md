# Summary

The selected weakness was a **front-door overcompression between glial substrate-routing, neuronal bioenergetic state, and human energetic / astrocyte-related proxy evidence**.

The repository already had stronger internal route-card language in the maintenance-state pages, but the paper-facing entry layer still made it too easy for a reader to stop at:

- `human energetic imaging exists`
- `astrocyte-related PET exists`
- `therefore the active glial fuel route is becoming visible`

The current primary literature does not support that promotion.

# Why This Needed Correction

## 1. Glial substrate-routing is already a route-family split, not one metabolic-support row

- [Suzuki et al. (2011)](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cell.2011.02.018) constrain a **lactate-shuttle support route**.
- [Silva et al. (2022)](https://doi.org/10.1038/s42255-022-00528-6) constrain a **glia-to-neuron ketone-body route under starvation**.
- [Pavlowsky et al. (2025)](https://doi.org/10.1038/s42255-025-01416-5) constrain a **glia-to-neuron fatty-acid route during intensive learning**.
- [Greda et al. (2025)](https://doi.org/10.1038/s42255-025-01389-5) constrain **apoE / sortilin-dependent neuronal lipid uptake and fuel-choice gating**.

Critique:

- These papers do not constrain one interchangeable `glial metabolism` object.
- They differ in supplier cell, fuel object, transport logic, and stress / learning regime.
- A front door that says only `metabolic support` teaches the wrong inferential object.

## 2. Human energetic proxy advances do not close the supplier-fuel-sink question

- [Ren et al. (2015)](https://doi.org/10.1002/nbm.3384) constrain **31P metabolite / pH balance**.
- [Ren et al. (2017)](https://doi.org/10.1002/mrm.26560) constrain **31P MT exchange-flux**.
- [Guo et al. (2024)](https://doi.org/10.3389/fnins.2024.1389111) constrain **whole-brain NAD-content mapping**.
- [Kaiser et al. (2026)](https://doi.org/10.1177/0271678X261415784) constrain **localized functional NAD-dynamics**.
- [Karkouri et al. (2026)](https://doi.org/10.1002/mrm.70308) constrain **deuterium absolute quantification**.
- [Li et al. (2025)](https://doi.org/10.1093/pnasnexus/pgaf072) constrain **deuterium kinetic-rate imaging**.

Critique:

- These are important human advances, but they remain **macro energetic proxy classes of different quantity types**.
- They do not directly say which glial supplier delivered which fuel through which transport route to which neuronal sink.
- Therefore they are not direct glial substrate-routing evidence.

## 3. Astrocyte-related PET is also not a route-free glial fuel-routing readout

- [Villemagne et al. (2022)](https://doi.org/10.2967/jnumed.121.263254) constrain **SMBT-1 first-in-human target validation**.
- [Matsuoka et al. (2026)](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00259-025-07542-2) constrain **SL25.1188 simplified-quantification in AD**.
- [Tyacke et al. (2018)](https://doi.org/10.2967/jnumed.118.208009) constrain an **I2BS target class**.

Critique:

- These are target-defined astrocyte-related proxy routes, not direct measurements of lactate, ketone, fatty-acid, or apoE-lipid routing.
- Even a strong astrocyte-related PET paper does not identify the active fuel object or neuronal sink.

## 4. The site needed to block a specific category error

Without a sharper front-door stop rule, a reader could silently make the following invalid upgrade:

- `human energetic proxy` -> `neuronal mitochondrial state`
- `astrocyte-related PET` -> `active glial fuel route`
- `macro metabolic map` -> `supplier-fuel-sink truth`

That overread weakens the site's central discipline of separating **what is causally shown in local models** from **what is boundedly observable in living humans**.

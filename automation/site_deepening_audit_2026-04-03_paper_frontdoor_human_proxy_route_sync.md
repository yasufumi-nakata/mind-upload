# Site Deepening Audit (2026-04-03, paper front-door human proxy route sync)

## Target

- Main public page:
  - `mind_uploading_papers.md`
- Scope:
  - Technology
  - Natural science
- Explicitly excluded:
  - Philosophy
  - Law

## Summary

- The deepest technical weakness remaining on the paper-archive front door was not missing volume. It was summary-layer compression.
- The page already had enough literature to justify a stricter route-family taxonomy for living-human evidence, but several high-traffic front-door sentences and table cells still collapsed distinct inferential objects into broader labels.
- I therefore chose the `mind_uploading_papers.md` front door itself as the next update target and tightened three linked summary layers:
  - spectroscopy
  - blood-CSF-barrier / choroid-plexus measurement
  - astrocyte PET

## Why This Needed Correction

- A reader entering through the paper collection page should not have to infer the real route split only after reading deeper notes.
- The prior front-door wording still allowed three scientifically incorrect shortcuts:
  - `1H-MRSI` as if parcel similarity and high-resolution metabolite distribution were one row
  - `blood-CSF barrier` as if perfusion, transport, water cycling, apparent exchange, and simultaneous BBB-versus-BCSFB estimation were one row
  - `astrocyte PET` as if SMBT-1, SL25.1188, and I2BS were interchangeable answers to one quantity
- That compression lowered the site’s own claim ceiling discipline. The deeper page already knew better; the entrance summary still taught the weaker model.

## Main Critique

### 1. Destructive structure and living-human proxy evidence still risked being re-collapsed at the entrance layer

- [Shapson-Coe et al. (2024)](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.adk4858), [Lu et al. (2023)](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.crmeth.2023.100520), [Dorkenwald et al. (2024)](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-024-07558-y), and [MICrONS Consortium et al. (2025)](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-025-08790-w) together make the same point from different angles: destructive ultrastructure and same-brain structure-function pipelines remain preservation-, registration-, and proofreading-limited.
- [Johansen et al. (2024)](https://doi.org/10.1523/JNEUROSCI.1750-23.2024) and the living-human MRI / PET papers do not solve the same observability problem. They belong to a different inferential ladder built from route-specific proxies.

Critique:

- The archive front door must stop readers before they silently convert `human papers exist` into `same-object progress`.
- The correct comparison is not `old destructive work` versus `new living-human work` on one common bar. It is `destructive local structure` versus `living-human route-specific proxies` under different burdens.

### 2. The spectroscopy lane is already internally split before any strong current-state reading is allowed

- [Lucchetti et al. (2025)](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-025-66124-w) is a `five-metabolite parcel-similarity scaffold`.
- [Guo et al. (2025)](https://doi.org/10.1109/TBME.2025.3572448) is a `high-resolution metabolite-distribution route` with explicit reconstruction burden.
- [Ren et al. (2015)](https://doi.org/10.1002/nbm.3384), [Ren et al. (2017)](https://doi.org/10.1002/mrm.26560), [Guo et al. (2024)](https://doi.org/10.3389/fnins.2024.1389111), and [Kaiser et al. (2026)](https://doi.org/10.1177/0271678X261415784) further show that the `31P` family itself already splits into balance, exchange-flux, NAD-content, and localized functional NAD-dynamics routes.
- [Karkouri et al. (2026)](https://doi.org/10.1002/mrm.70308) and [Li et al. (2025)](https://doi.org/10.1093/pnasnexus/pgaf072) show that deuterium work also splits into `absolute quantification` versus `kinetic-rate imaging`.
- [Ahmadian et al. (2025)](https://doi.org/10.1002/jmri.29532) and [Bøgh et al. (2024)](https://doi.org/10.1186/s41747-024-00426-4) show that deuterium interpretation still depends on dose, time-point, and repeatability regime.

Critique:

- `Spectroscopy` is not one route family at the archive entrance.
- Even after the route family is fixed, the deuterium slice still needs operating-point disclosure.
- Therefore the front door must explicitly separate:
  - five-metabolite `1H-MRSI` similarity
  - high-resolution `1H-MRSI` metabolite distribution
  - `31P` balance / exchange-flux / NAD-content / localized NAD-dynamics
  - deuterium absolute quantification
  - deuterium kinetic imaging plus operating burden

### 3. The blood-CSF-barrier lane was still too compressed for the current evidence base

- [Zhao et al. (2020)](https://doi.org/10.1186/s12987-020-00218-z) measures `choroid-plexus apparent blood flow`.
- [Petitclerc et al. (2021)](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.neuroimage.2021.118755) measures `blood-to-CSF water transport`.
- [Anderson et al. (2022)](https://doi.org/10.1080/21688370.2021.1963143) addresses `in vivo water cycling at the human choroid plexus`.
- [Wu et al. (2026)](https://doi.org/10.1177/0271678X251369218) addresses `apparent BCSFB water exchange` in a feasibility framing.
- [Petitclerc et al. (2026)](https://doi.org/10.1177/0271678X261429042) estimates water transport across `BBB` and `BCSFB` simultaneously rather than treating them as one scalar.

Critique:

- A single `blood-CSF barrier` phrase hides at least five non-interchangeable rows:
  - choroid-plexus perfusion
  - blood-to-CSF transport
  - DCE water cycling
  - apparent BCSFB exchange
  - simultaneous BBB-versus-BCSFB exchange
- Compressing them into one entrance label makes the site easier to overread than the primary literature warrants.

### 4. Astrocyte PET also needed route-role separation at the summary layer

- [Villemagne et al. (2022a)](https://doi.org/10.2967/jnumed.121.263254) provides first-in-human `SMBT-1` target validation and quantification framing.
- [Villemagne et al. (2022b)](https://doi.org/10.2967/jnumed.121.263255) provides the `AD-spectrum` context for `SMBT-1`.
- [Hiraoka et al. (2025)](https://doi.org/10.1007/s12149-025-02083-y) shows that `SMBT-1` itself splits again by brain quantification regime.
- [Mesfin et al. (2026)](https://doi.org/10.1007/s12149-025-02144-2) adds whole-body biodistribution / dosimetry rather than another brain-state row.
- [Tyacke et al. (2018)](https://doi.org/10.2967/jnumed.118.208009) anchors the `I2BS` route as a different astrocyte-related target family.
- [Matsuoka et al. (2026)](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00259-025-07542-2) and [Best et al. (2026)](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41380-025-03355-9) show that `SL25.1188` also has route-local quantification and cohort-severity burdens.

Critique:

- `Astrocyte PET` is not one current-state meter.
- The archive entrance must disclose at least the route-role split between:
  - `SMBT-1` target-validation / AD-context / brain-quantification / whole-body-biodistribution
  - `SL25.1188` simplified-quantification / severity-conditioned MAO-B
  - `I2BS`

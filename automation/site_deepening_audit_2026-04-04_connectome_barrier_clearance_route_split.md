# Summary

The selected weakness was a **central-page overcompression of human barrier-side and clearance-side observability** inside [`wiki/connectome-is-not-enough.md`](/Users/yasufumi/Documents/GitHub/mind-upload/wiki/connectome-is-not-enough.md).

The page was already correct about the high-level claim:

- connectome-complete is not emulation-complete
- neurovascular / BBB / pericyte state remains separate
- clearance / immune support remains separate

But its **human-side stop line** had become scientifically weaker than the rest of the site.

Other public pages had already moved to the stricter rule:

- `BBB water-exchange` is not `tracer-specific BBB transport`
- `BBB` is not `blood-CSF barrier / choroid-plexus transport`
- `CSF oscillation` is not `parenchyma-CSF exchange`
- `intrathecal-tracer / CSF-to-blood clearance` is not `CSF mobility`
- `CSF mobility` is not `model-based biomarker efflux`

The central connectome page still let those human rows sound closer than the primary literature permits.

# Why This Needed Correction

## 1. The central concept page had fallen behind the route-family rule already used elsewhere

[`tech_roadmap.md`](/Users/yasufumi/Documents/GitHub/mind-upload/tech_roadmap.md), [`wbe_101.md`](/Users/yasufumi/Documents/GitHub/mind-upload/wbe_101.md), and [`wiki/measurement-stack-and-claim-ceiling.md`](/Users/yasufumi/Documents/GitHub/mind-upload/wiki/measurement-stack-and-claim-ceiling.md) already treated human support-state observability as **family-internal splits**, not one generic improvement in “seeing the brain”.

The connectome page did not yet follow that rule strongly enough in its own barrier-side and clearance-side stop lines.

That matters because this page is not a side note.

It is the page that answers:

- why wiring alone is insufficient
- which missing families remain latent
- what kinds of human evidence do or do not shrink those latent families

If this page compresses route families, it teaches exactly the shortcut that the rest of the site is designed to prevent.

## 2. Human barrier-side evidence is not one `BBB proxy`

- [Morgan et al. (2024)](https://doi.org/10.1002/nbm.5256) constrain a **BBB water-exchange** route under ASL method choices.
- [Padrela et al. (2025)](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.neurobiolaging.2024.12.012) extend that **BBB water-exchange** family across the adult lifespan.
- [Chung et al. (2025)](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-025-58356-7) constrain **tracer-specific BBB transport** with dynamic PET and kinetic modeling.
- [Zhao et al. (2020)](https://doi.org/10.1186/s12987-020-00218-z) constrain **choroid-plexus perfusion**.
- [Petitclerc et al. (2021)](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.neuroimage.2021.118755) constrain **blood-to-CSF water transport**.
- [Petitclerc et al. (2026)](https://doi.org/10.1177/0271678X261429042) constrain **simultaneous BBB-versus-BCSFB exchange**.

Critique:

- these papers do not share one crossed boundary
- they do not share one carrier or transport object
- they do not share one model family
- they do not share one validation ceiling

Therefore a page that says only `human BBB proxy` is teaching the wrong inferential object.

## 3. Human clearance evidence is not one `glymphatic route`

- [Fultz et al. (2019)](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.aax5440) constrain **macroscopic CSF oscillation** during sleep.
- [Kim, Huang, & Liu (2025)](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.neuroimage.2025.121142) constrain **parenchyma-CSF water exchange**.
- [Eide et al. (2023)](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-023-37685-5) constrain an **intrathecal-tracer / CSF-to-blood-clearance-capacity** route.
- [Hirschler et al. (2025)](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41593-025-02073-3) constrain **CSF mobility**.
- [Dagum et al. (2026)](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-026-68374-8) constrain a **model-based overnight biomarker-efflux** route.

Critique:

- these are different transport objects
- these run on different time windows
- these carry different model burdens
- these do not become one route merely because they all touch CSF or clearance

Therefore a page that says only `human glymphatic / CSF route` is again teaching the wrong object.

## 4. The page also needed to keep human proxy routes separate from controller-side biology

- [Bell et al. (2010)](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.neuron.2010.09.043), [Kisler et al. (2020)](https://doi.org/10.3389/fncel.2020.00027), [Pandey et al. (2023)](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.neuron.2023.08.030), and [Mai-Morente et al. (2025)](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-025-61312-0) constrain controller-side pericyte / neurovascular biology.
- [Louveau et al. (2015)](https://doi.org/10.1038/nature14432), [Ahn et al. (2019)](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-019-1419-5), and [Kim et al. (2025)](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cell.2025.02.022) constrain drainage anatomy and microglia-linked synaptic physiology.

Those local causal papers matter because they show that:

- the controller-side biology is real
- but the current human routes are still indirect, route-bounded, and family-split

If the connectome page compresses the human rows, it becomes too easy to slide from:

- `the controller matters`
- `a human route exists`

to:

- `the controller is now measured in humans`

The primary literature does not support that upgrade.

# Root Critique

The scientific criticism is precise:

1. The site already adopted `route family first` for human support-state evidence.
2. The central connectome page still under-specified that rule exactly where readers most need it.
3. This was not a wording preference. It was a real category error.
4. `BBB water-exchange`, `tracer-specific BBB transport`, and `BCSFB / choroid-plexus routes` are not one barrier row.
5. `CSF oscillation`, `parenchyma-CSF exchange`, `intrathecal-tracer clearance`, `CSF mobility`, and `model-based biomarker efflux` are not one clearance row.
6. Therefore the page had to be corrected so that its stop lines match the current primary literature and the rest of the site's verification logic.

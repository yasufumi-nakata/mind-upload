## Why This Topic Was Chosen

- The core site had already become much stricter about two technical stop lines:
  - `same-brain functional connectomics != solved local twin`
  - `closed-loop timing != solved deployability or solved body/environment boundary`
- However, the two public literature-entry pages still undernamed those stop lines relative to the core pages.
- That mismatch is dangerous because these two pages are exactly where technically oriented readers decide which papers count as frontier evidence before they open the more detailed route-card pages.

## Evidence-Based Critique

### 1. The literature front door still left same-brain functional connectomics too compressible

- `same-brain`, `functional connectomics`, `digital twin`, `predicted transcriptomic type`, `current synaptic state`, and `unique dynamics` are not the same claim.
- Primary literature does not support compressing them into one route:
  - [Bosch et al. (2022)](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-022-30199-6) showed a multistage landmark-based bridge from in vivo physiology to volume EM.
  - [MICrONS Consortium et al. (2025)](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-025-08790-w) strengthened same-brain local structure-function linkage, but still through a sequential pipeline.
  - [Ding et al. (2025)](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-025-08840-3) added a validated stimulus-conditioned response model.
  - [Gamlin et al. (2025)](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-025-08805-6) still used morphology-based prediction to transfer transcriptomic labels.
  - [Holler et al. (2021)](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-020-03134-2), [Dürst et al. (2022)](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-022-33565-6), and [Mittermaier et al. (2024)](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-024-53901-2) keep current synaptic efficacy, release probability, and membrane-state-gated consolidation separate from structure alone.
  - [Beiran & Litwin-Kumar (2025)](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41593-025-02080-4) show that connectome-constrained dynamics can remain degenerate until extra recordings collapse the compatible family.
- Therefore, the safe literature-front-door reading is:
  - `sequential local scaffold`
  - `task-bounded conditional predictor`
- It is not:
  - `direct transcriptomic truth`
  - `current synaptic-state readout`
  - `one solved local twin`

### 2. The literature front door still left closed-loop evidence too timing-centric

- `low latency`, `low jitter`, `fixed-decoder interval`, and `recalibration burden` are necessary audits, but they do not exhaust the biological operating regime.
- Primary literature shows that slow internal-milieu variables can change memory or plasticity even when the visible fast I/O loop is unchanged:
  - [de Quervain et al. (1998)](https://doi.org/10.1038/29542) showed glucocorticoid-dependent retrieval impairment.
  - [Oei et al. (2007)](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11682-007-9003-2) showed hydrocortisone-linked decreases in human hippocampal and prefrontal retrieval activity.
  - [McCauley et al. (2020)](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.celrep.2020.108255), [Barone et al. (2023)](https://doi.org/10.1126/sciadv.adj1010), and [Birnie et al. (2023)](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2211996120) showed circadian and corticosteroid timing effects on hippocampal plasticity machinery.
  - [Reger et al. (2008)](https://doi.org/10.3233/JAD-2008-13309) and [Sherman et al. (2015)](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.neuropsychologia.2015.07.020) showed that insulin signaling and circadian-rhythm consistency can shift human memory or hippocampal activity.
- Therefore, the safe literature-front-door reading for `closed-loop deployability` must require:
  - timing / instability metrics
  - plus explicit slow internal-milieu disclosure
- It is not enough to say that timing was good and the decoder survived a short interval.
